## Projects

Three projects, all public.

### [Codeboros](https://github.com/CouraLabs/Codeboros)
*Self-hosted coding agent* · Pre-release: v1 is still being built

- Reviews your codebase and pull requests as often as you tell it to.
- Reproduces a reported bug first, before it says anything is fixed.
- Then it tests its own fix in a real browser and records the video.
- A named person reviews every fix before it can merge.

**Stack:** Bun, TypeScript, Docker Compose · **License:** Apache 2.0 · **Runs:** on your infrastructure, with your model keys

### [OpenMixing](https://github.com/CouraLabs/OpenMixing)
*MCP server that lets your agent DJ* · Usable now, installed from source

- Point your agent at a folder of tracks.
- It works out tempo, key, energy and sections for every track.
- Then it builds a set: EQ, filters, tempo changes, effects, loops.
- The mix renders offline, and a local player shows every move it made.

**Stack:** Python, MCP over stdio, ffmpeg · **License:** GPL 3.0 · **Runs:** locally, on your own files

### [OpenHouse](https://github.com/CouraLabs/OpenHouse)
*Household finance with shared books* · Free to self-host today

- One shared ledger that everyone in the house signs in to.
- Accounts and cards in USD, BRL and EUR, side by side.
- Recurring rules, category limits and savings goals.
- Hosted plans are planned, and one of them will have AI features.

**Stack:** PostgreSQL, NestJS, React · **Self-host:** `docker compose up` · **Runs:** on your server, or hosted later
